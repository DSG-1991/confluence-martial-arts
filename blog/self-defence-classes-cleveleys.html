Why Traditional Martial Arts for Self-Defence?

There is a common misconception that traditional martial arts are outdated or impractical for modern self-defence. Nothing could be further from the truth. The techniques preserved in Daito-ryu jujutsu were developed and refined over centuries of real combat. They were not designed for sport, competition, or point-scoring — they were designed to end a confrontation as quickly and decisively as possible.

Here is what makes Daito-ryu jujutsu particularly effective for self-defence:

It Does Not Rely on Strength

Daito-ryu techniques are based on leverage, angles, and body mechanics — not on brute force. A smaller, weaker person can control a larger, stronger attacker by using proper technique. This is why the art has traditionally been taught to people of all sizes, including women and older students. If you are worried that you are not strong enough for self-defence, Daito-ryu is exactly what you need.

It Covers All Ranges of Attack

Most self-defence situations do not start with a formal fighting stance. They start with a grab, a push, or someone getting in your face. Daito-ryu jujutsu specialises in exactly these scenarios. You will learn how to escape from wrist grabs, collar grabs, bear hugs, headlocks, and chokes. You will learn how to defend yourself if you are pushed to the ground. And you will learn how to use strikes to vulnerable points if the situation escalates.

It Teaches Control, Not Just Violence

One of the most valuable aspects of Daito-ryu is that it gives you options. A joint lock can control an aggressive individual without causing lasting injury — which is important from both a legal and moral standpoint. You learn to match your response to the level of threat, rather than defaulting to striking.

What You Will Learn in Our Self-Defence Classes

Our classes are structured to build real, usable skills. Here is a breakdown of the core areas we cover:

Escape and Evasion

The first priority in any self-defence situation is to create distance and escape. You will learn how to break common grabs and holds, how to create space, and how to move to safety. These are the skills most likely to be useful in everyday situations.

Joint Locks and Controls

When escape is not possible, joint locks allow you to control an attacker. You will learn wrist locks, elbow locks, and shoulder controls that can subdue an aggressor without striking. These techniques are precise and mechanical — they work because of how the human body is built, not because of how strong you are.

Throws and Takedowns

If you need to put an attacker on the ground, Daito-ryu offers a range of throws that use the attacker's momentum against them. These are not the high-amplitude throws you see in judo competition — they are practical, low-risk takedowns designed to end a confrontation safely.

Ground Defence

If you end up on the ground — which is common in real altercations — you need to know how to protect yourself, create space, and get back to your feet. Our curriculum includes practical ground defence techniques that prioritise safety and escape.

Situational Awareness

Physical technique is only one part of self-defence. We also teach situational awareness — how to read a situation, how to de-escalate conflict verbally, and how to avoid dangerous situations before they develop. The best self-defence is the one you never have to use.

Self-Defence on the Fylde Coast

While the Fylde Coast is generally a safe place to live, no community is immune to incidents. Like any area, Cleveleys, Blackpool, and the surrounding towns have their share of late-night disturbances, anti-social behaviour, and situations where knowing how to handle yourself can make all the difference.

Our students come from all walks of life — professionals, students, parents, retirees. Some want to feel safer walking home after a late shift. Others want to be able to protect their children. Some simply want the confidence that comes with knowing they are not helpless. Whatever your reason, you are welcome here.

Who Are Our Self-Defence Classes For?

Our classes are open to everyone aged 14 and above. You do not need any previous experience, and you do not need to be fit. We have students who are complete beginners and students who have trained in other martial arts for years. Everyone trains together, learns from each other, and progresses at their own pace.

We particularly welcome:

  • Women looking for practical, effective self-defence skills
  • Older adults who want to stay active and confident
  • Teenagers building confidence and awareness
  • Anyone who has ever felt vulnerable and wants to do something about it
