Takeuchi Renshinkan Training on the Fylde Coast — A Living Tradition

Craig Abernethy August 5, 2026 8 min read
Takeuchi Renshinkan — a comprehensive traditional Japanese martial system

If you have been searching for Takeuchi Renshinkan UK or traditional Japanese martial arts UK, you may have noticed how rare authentic instruction in these arts truly is. Unlike the more common modern martial arts — karate, judo, taekwondo — Takeuchi Renshinkan is a classical koryu (old school) martial system that has been preserved and passed down through generations in an unbroken lineage.

At Confluence Martial Arts in Thornton-Cleveleys, we are proud to offer Takeuchi Renshinkan training to students across the Fylde Coast. It is a rare opportunity to study a martial art that traces its origins back over 400 years to the Warring States period of Japan.

What Is Takeuchi Renshinkan?

Takeuchi Renshinkan (竹内練心館) is a branch of the Takeuchi-ryu tradition, one of the oldest documented Japanese martial systems in existence. Founded by Takeuchi Hisamori in 1532, Takeuchi-ryu is widely recognised as the oldest jujutsu school in Japan. The Renshinkan branch was established later to preserve and systematise the teachings for modern practitioners while maintaining the classical curriculum.

What makes Takeuchi Renshinkan unique is its comprehensive nature. Unlike many modern martial arts that focus on a single area — striking, grappling, or weapons — Takeuchi Renshinkan is a complete martial system that covers all aspects of combat as they were understood by the samurai class.

What You Will Learn

Training in Takeuchi Renshinkan is a journey through the full spectrum of traditional Japanese martial arts. The curriculum is broad and deep, offering something for every martial artist:

Jujutsu (Unarmed Combat)

The core of the system. You will learn throws, joint locks, pins, and strikes — but with a distinctly classical flavour. Takeuchi-ryu jujutsu techniques were designed for armoured and unarmoured combat alike, giving them a unique character that differs from later, sport-oriented styles. The techniques are practical, direct, and highly effective.

Weaponry (Buki-waza)

Takeuchi Renshinkan includes training in a range of traditional weapons. You will study the tachi (long sword), kodachi (short sword), jo (short staff), and other classical weapons. This weapon training is not separate from the unarmed techniques — it informs and enhances them. Understanding how a sword cuts and how to evade it gives your empty-hand techniques a new level of depth and precision.

Self-Defence (Goshin-waza)

The system includes a dedicated self-defence curriculum that addresses common attack scenarios — grabs, chokes, strikes, and weapon threats. These techniques are practical and adaptable, designed to work in the real world regardless of your size or strength.

Kata (Forms)

Structured sequences of techniques that preserve the system's knowledge. Kata in Takeuchi Renshinkan are not abstract dances — they are living records of combat principles, each movement with a clear martial application. Practising kata develops timing, distance, and the ability to flow between techniques seamlessly.

How Takeuchi Renshinkan Complements Daito-ryu Jujutsu

At Confluence Martial Arts, we teach both Daito-ryu jujutsu and Takeuchi Renshinkan. These two arts complement each other beautifully, and training in both gives you a uniquely well-rounded martial education.

Daito-ryu jujutsu focuses primarily on unarmed joint locks, throws, and strikes, with an emphasis on controlling an opponent's energy and momentum. It is subtle, sophisticated, and deeply technical — an art of refinement and precision.

Takeuchi Renshinkan broadens the picture. It adds weapon training, a different approach to jujutsu, and a historical perspective that connects you to the martial traditions of the samurai. Where Daito-ryu might focus on the fine details of a single wrist lock, Takeuchi Renshinkan shows you how that same principle applies when an opponent is armed with a sword.

Together, they give you a complete martial education that is rare in the UK and almost unheard of on the Fylde Coast.

Why the Fylde Coast Is Lucky to Have This Training

Authentic koryu martial arts are not easy to find in the UK. Most traditional Japanese martial arts schools teach modern, sport-oriented versions of arts like judo, karate, or aikido. While these are valuable in their own right, they are a far cry from the classical systems that preserve the full combat traditions of feudal Japan.

Takeuchi Renshinkan, in particular, is rare. There are only a handful of instructors in the UK qualified to teach it, and even fewer who teach it alongside Daito-ryu jujutsu. The fact that this training is available in Thornton-Cleveleys — a short drive from Blackpool, Fleetwood, Poulton, and Lytham — is genuinely remarkable.

Craig Abernethy, your instructor, has trained extensively in both arts. His time at the Inosanto Academy in Marina del Rey, California, under Guru Dan Inosanto, gave him exposure to a wide range of martial traditions and a deep appreciation for the value of classical training. His approach is rooted in authenticity but delivered in a way that is accessible to modern students.

Who Is Takeuchi Renshinkan For?

Takeuchi Renshinkan is suitable for anyone with an interest in traditional Japanese martial arts. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced martial artist looking to deepen your understanding, you will find value in this system.

It is particularly rewarding for:

  • Martial artists who want to explore the roots of jujutsu and understand where modern techniques come from
  • History enthusiasts who want to connect with the living traditions of feudal Japan
  • Weapons enthusiasts who want to study classical Japanese weaponry in a structured, authentic context
  • Complete beginners who want to start their martial arts journey with something deep and meaningful

No previous experience is required. The techniques are taught progressively, and you will be guided through the curriculum at a pace that suits you. The only prerequisites are an open mind and a willingness to learn.
