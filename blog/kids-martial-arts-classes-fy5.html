Kids Martial Arts Classes FY5 — Building Confidence and Discipline

Craig Abernethy August 5, 2026 7 min read
Confluence Kids — learning traditional martial arts in a fun, structured environment

If you are a parent living in the FY5 postcode area — covering Thornton-Cleveleys, Blackpool, and the surrounding Fylde Coast — you have probably thought about enrolling your child in martial arts. Maybe you want them to build confidence, learn discipline, or simply burn off some energy in a productive way. Whatever your reason, kids martial arts classes FY5 are more accessible than you might think.

At Confluence Martial Arts, our Confluence Kids programme introduces children to traditional Japanese martial arts in a fun, safe, and structured environment. We are based at Cleveleys Citizens Hall, right in the heart of Thornton-Cleveleys, making it easy for families across the FY5 area to attend.

The Benefits of Martial Arts for Children

Martial arts offers children far more than just physical activity. The benefits extend into every area of their lives — from the classroom to the playground, and from their relationships to their own sense of self-worth.

Confidence and Self-Esteem

One of the most noticeable changes in children who train regularly is a growth in confidence. As they master new techniques, earn recognition for their progress, and learn to perform in front of others, their self-esteem grows naturally. This is not the hollow confidence of empty praise — it is the genuine confidence that comes from developing real skill.

Discipline and Focus

Traditional martial arts are built on respect and structure. Children learn to listen, follow instructions, and concentrate on the task at hand. These skills transfer directly to the classroom, where teachers often notice improved attention and behaviour in children who train.

Physical Fitness

In an age of screens and sedentary lifestyles, martial arts gets children moving in a way that feels like play. They develop strength, flexibility, coordination, and cardiovascular fitness — all while having fun. Unlike many sports, martial arts engages the whole body and mind, keeping children interested and motivated.

Respect and Social Skills

Bowing to partners, waiting your turn, helping a fellow student who is struggling — these are built into every class. Children learn to respect their instructors, their peers, and themselves. They also make friends in a positive, supportive environment.

Self-Defence Awareness

While we never want children to be in situations where they need to defend themselves, we believe every child should have the basic awareness and skills to stay safe. Our classes teach situational awareness, how to set boundaries, and age-appropriate physical techniques for escaping common grabs and holds.

What Confluence Kids Classes Look Like

Our kids classes are structured to be engaging, educational, and — most importantly — fun. We know that children learn best when they are enjoying themselves, so every session balances instruction with activity.

A typical class includes:

  • Warm-up games that build coordination and get everyone moving
  • Basic technique practice — simple strikes, blocks, and stances from traditional Japanese martial arts
  • Partner drills — learning to work with a partner safely and respectfully
  • Kata and forms — structured sequences that build memory, focus, and precision
  • Cool-down and reflection — ending each class with a moment to settle and absorb what was learned

We also introduce elements of Jissen Karate-do Renshinkan, giving children exposure to striking and kata alongside the jujutsu curriculum. This variety keeps classes fresh and gives each child multiple ways to engage with the material.

Addressing Parent Concerns

We understand that as a parent, you have questions. Here are the ones we hear most often:

Is it safe?

Safety is our absolute priority. All techniques are taught in a controlled, progressive manner. Children are matched with partners of similar size and ability. We maintain a low student-to-instructor ratio so every child gets individual attention. The hall is a safe, clean, well-lit space.

What age range do you accept?

Our Confluence Kids programme is designed for children from approximately 6 to 14 years old. We assess each child individually and can adjust the class experience to suit their maturity and ability level.

Does my child need any experience?

None at all. Many of our students start as complete beginners. We teach everything from the ground up, and children progress at their own pace. There is no pressure to keep up with anyone else.

What does it cost?

We keep our pricing affordable and transparent. The best way to find out is to come for a free taster session — no obligation, no commitment. Just bring your child along in comfortable clothing and let them experience a class first-hand.

What should my child wear?

For the first few sessions, comfortable sportswear (tracksuit bottoms and a t-shirt) is perfectly fine. If they decide to continue, we can help you source a suitable uniform.

Why Choose Confluence Martial Arts for Your Child?

There are plenty of activities for children in the FY5 area, so why choose traditional martial arts at Confluence?

Because we offer something genuinely different. Our classes are not about competition, trophies, or belt factories. We teach real martial arts with real substance. Your child will learn skills that will serve them for life — not just in the dojo, but in how they carry themselves, how they treat others, and how they face challenges.

Craig Abernethy, your instructor, has trained at the Inosanto Academy in California and brings a depth of knowledge that is rare in children's martial arts. But more importantly, he has a genuine passion for teaching young people and helping them discover their potential.

"Every child has something special to offer. Our job as instructors is to create an environment where they feel safe enough to discover it, and challenged enough to grow."

— Craig Abernethy, Confluence Martial Arts
