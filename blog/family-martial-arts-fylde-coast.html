Family Martial Arts on the Fylde Coast — Training Together at Confluence

Craig Abernethy August 5, 2026 7 min read
Confluence Martial Arts — a welcoming community for families across the Fylde Coast

If you are looking for family martial arts on the Fylde Coast, you may be searching for something that goes beyond a typical kids' class. Perhaps you want to train alongside your children, sharing an activity that benefits everyone. Or maybe you are looking for a martial arts school where every member of the family — regardless of age — can find their place.

At Confluence Martial Arts in Thornton-Cleveleys, we welcome families. Our classes are designed to accommodate students of all ages, from children taking their first steps in martial arts to adults who want to learn something meaningful. And because we teach both Daito-ryu jujutsu and Takeuchi Renshinkan, there is depth enough to keep every family member engaged for years to come.

Why Families Train Together

There is something special about sharing a martial arts journey with your family. When parents and children train at the same venue, martial arts becomes a shared language — something you talk about on the way home, practice in the living room, and grow in together.

Here are some of the benefits families discover when they train together at Confluence:

Shared Experience, Shared Growth

When your child learns a new technique and you understand exactly what it involves because you have practised it yourself, the connection deepens. You can encourage each other, celebrate each other's progress, and work through challenges together. Martial arts becomes a family activity rather than just another after-school club.

Consistency and Commitment

Families who train together find it easier to stay consistent. When everyone is heading to the same venue at the same time, there is no juggling multiple drop-offs or conflicting schedules. You arrive together, train in your respective classes, and leave together. It is efficient, enjoyable, and builds a healthy routine for the whole family.

Mutual Respect

There is a unique dynamic that develops when a child sees their parent learning something new — struggling with a technique, asking questions, and persevering. It models the very values that martial arts teaches: humility, patience, and the willingness to be a beginner. Children learn that their parents are also students, and that learning never stops.

Martial Arts for All Ages at Confluence

One of the most common questions we hear from families is whether both Daito-ryu jujutsu and Takeuchi Renshinkan are suitable for different age groups. The answer is yes — both arts are taught in a way that respects the needs of each student, whether they are eight or eighty.

Confluence Kids (Ages 6–14)

Our children's classes introduce young students to traditional Japanese martial arts in a fun, structured environment. The focus is on building confidence, discipline, coordination, and respect. Children learn basic techniques from both Daito-ryu and Takeuchi Renshinkan, including simple joint controls, strikes, stances, and age-appropriate self-defence awareness. Classes are energetic, engaging, and designed to keep young minds focused and motivated.

Adult Classes (Ages 14+)

Our adult classes cover the full depth of the Daito-ryu and Takeuchi Renshinkan curricula. Whether you are a complete beginner or have trained in other arts, you will find a progressive learning path that challenges you physically and mentally. Adults appreciate the technical depth, the historical context, and the practical self-defence applications. The class environment is supportive — there is no competition, no pressure to perform, just genuine learning at your own pace.

Mixed-Ability Training

Because our classes are structured around partner drills and progressive technique work, students of different abilities train together productively. Beginners learn from more experienced students, and experienced students deepen their understanding by teaching. This creates a community where everyone supports everyone else — and that is especially powerful when the community includes your own family.

The Community Aspect

Beyond the techniques and the training, what makes Confluence Martial Arts special is the community. Families who train with us often tell us that the dojo feels like an extended family. Parents chat while waiting for classes. Children make friends who share their interest in martial arts. Students celebrate each other's progress, whether it is mastering a difficult technique or simply showing up consistently.

This sense of community is especially valuable on the Fylde Coast, where families are looking for meaningful, screen-free activities that bring people together. In a world where so much of our lives is digital and disconnected, training at a traditional martial arts dojo offers something real — a place where you show up, move your body, learn with your hands, and connect with people face to face.

Why Choose Confluence for Your Family?

There are many activities available for families on the Fylde Coast, but traditional Japanese martial arts offer something unique. Here is why families choose Confluence:

  • Two arts in one place — Daito-ryu jujutsu and Takeuchi Renshinkan provide a rich, varied curriculum that keeps training fresh and engaging for years
  • All ages, one venue — no need to drive to different locations for different family members
  • Authentic instruction — Craig Abernethy has trained at the Inosanto Academy in California and brings genuine lineage-based teaching to the Fylde Coast
  • Progressive learning — structured curricula that allow each family member to progress at their own pace
  • Real skills for life — self-defence, confidence, discipline, and fitness that benefit every age group

Location and Practical Details

Our classes are held at Cleveleys Citizens Hall, FY5 3NG, in Thornton-Cleveleys. The venue is easily accessible from Blackpool, Fleetwood, Poulton-le-Fylde, Lytham St Annes, and all parts of the Fylde Coast. There is free parking available, and the hall is fully accessible.

For your first class, wear comfortable clothing you can move in — tracksuit bottoms and a t-shirt are perfect. No special equipment is needed. Just bring yourself, your family, and an open mind.

"Some of the best moments in the dojo are watching a parent and child train together. There is a mutual respect that develops — the child sees the parent as a student too, and the parent sees the child in a new light. It is a beautiful thing to witness."

— Craig Abernethy, Confluence Martial Arts
