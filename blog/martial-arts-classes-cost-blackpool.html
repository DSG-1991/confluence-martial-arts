Martial Arts Classes Cost in Blackpool — Affordable Training at Confluence Martial Arts

Craig Abernethy August 5, 2026 7 min read
Adult martial arts training at Cleveleys Citizens Hall — value that goes far beyond the price

If you have been searching for martial arts classes cost in Blackpool, you have probably noticed that prices vary widely. Some clubs charge premium rates for trendy fitness-based programmes. Others offer cut-price deals that make you wonder what you are actually getting. At Confluence Martial Arts, we believe in being straightforward about pricing — and even more importantly, about value.

This article will give you an honest picture of what it costs to train with us, what you get for your money, and why we believe traditional martial arts training is one of the best investments you can make in yourself.

What Is Included in Your Membership

When you train at Confluence Martial Arts, your membership covers more than just a class. Here is what is included:

Both Martial Arts, One Membership

Unlike many clubs that charge separately for different styles, your membership at Confluence gives you access to both Daito-ryu jujutsu and Takeuchi Renshinkan. These two arts complement each other perfectly — Daito-ryu develops your joint locks, throws, and strikes, while Takeuchi Renshinkan adds weaponry, historical perspective, and a broader martial education. You do not need to choose between them or pay extra to train both.

All Ages, One Price

We welcome students of all ages, from children in our Confluence Kids programme to adults of any age. Family members can train at the same venue, making it convenient and cost-effective for families who want to train together.

Structured Curriculum

Your membership gives you access to a progressive, structured curriculum based on the traditional Hiden-mokuroku scroll and the Takeuchi Renshinkan system. You are not just turning up to random classes — you are following a learning path that builds skill layer by layer, from beginner fundamentals to advanced techniques.

Expert Instruction

Craig Abernethy, your instructor, has trained at the Inosanto Academy in Marina del Rey, California, with Guru Dan Inosanto. His depth of knowledge in both Daito-ryu jujutsu and Takeuchi Renshinkan is rare in the UK. When you train at Confluence, you are learning from someone who has studied at one of the most respected martial arts institutions in the world.

How Our Pricing Compares to Other Activities

To put our pricing in perspective, let us compare it to other common activities and services in the Blackpool and Fylde Coast area:

  • Gym membership — A standard gym membership in Blackpool typically costs £25–£40 per month. You get access to equipment, but no instruction, no community, and no structured learning.
  • Personal training — One-to-one personal training sessions cost £30–£50 per hour. Effective, but expensive for regular sessions.
  • Other martial arts clubs — Many martial arts clubs in the area charge £30–£60 per month for a single style. Some add extra fees for gradings, competitions, or specialised classes.
  • Team sports — Football, rugby, and other team sports often have membership fees, kit costs, and travel expenses that add up quickly.

At Confluence Martial Arts, our pricing is competitive with — and often lower than — these alternatives. And when you consider that your membership includes two martial arts, expert instruction, and a welcoming community, the value becomes clear.

No Long-Term Contracts

We do not believe in locking people into long-term contracts. Life is unpredictable, and we understand that circumstances change. Our membership is straightforward — you pay for your training on a basis that works for you, with no hidden fees or penalties if you need to pause or stop.

This approach reflects our philosophy: we want people to train with us because they want to, not because they are contractually obligated. We believe that when you experience the value of the training, you will want to continue — and our students consistently do.

First Class Options

We know that the best way to decide if martial arts is right for you is to try it. That is why we offer flexible options for your first class. You can come along, meet the instructor, experience a class, and see how it feels before making any commitment.

For your first session, simply wear comfortable clothing — tracksuit bottoms and a t-shirt are perfect. No special equipment is needed. We will guide you through everything you need to know.

Is Martial Arts Worth the Investment?

This is the most important question, and it deserves an honest answer. The cost of martial arts training is not just a monthly payment — it is an investment in yourself. Here is what that investment returns:

Physical Health

Regular martial arts training improves cardiovascular fitness, strength, flexibility, and coordination. It is a full-body workout that engages your mind as well as your body. Compared to a gym membership where you might spend an hour on machines, martial arts training is more engaging, more varied, and more likely to keep you coming back.

Mental Resilience

Martial arts teaches you to stay calm under pressure, to solve problems in real time, and to persevere through difficulty. These skills transfer to every area of your life — work, relationships, and personal challenges.

Practical Self-Defence

The ability to protect yourself and your loved ones is something that cannot be priced. While we hope you never need to use these skills, the confidence that comes from knowing you have them is invaluable.

Community and Belonging

At Confluence, you are not just a member — you are part of a community. The friendships, the shared experiences, and the support of fellow students are a significant part of what makes training worthwhile.

"The value of martial arts training cannot be measured in pounds and pence. It is an investment in your health, your confidence, and your ability to handle whatever life throws at you. When you consider what you get in return, the cost is remarkably small."

— Craig Abernethy, Confluence Martial Arts
