Martial Arts for Beginners in Blackpool — It Is Never Too Late to Start

Craig Abernethy August 5, 2026 8 min read
Adult students training Daito-ryu jujutsu at Cleveleys Citizens Hall

If you are an adult searching for martial arts for beginners in Blackpool, you might be feeling a mix of curiosity and hesitation. Maybe you have always wanted to try martial arts but never got around to it. Maybe you think you are too old, too unfit, or too inflexible. Maybe you worry about looking foolish in front of people who have been training for years.

Let us put your mind at ease: every single person who walks through the doors of Confluence Martial Arts started exactly where you are now. And we have designed our classes to make that first step as welcoming and rewarding as possible.

You Are Not Too Old, Too Unfit, or Too Inflexible

This is the most common concern we hear from prospective adult students, and it is the one we most want to address. Traditional Japanese martial arts like Daito-ryu jujutsu and Takeuchi Renshinkan are not about who can do the most press-ups or touch their toes. They are about learning principles of movement, leverage, timing, and control.

Our classes are structured so that you progress at your own pace. You will not be thrown into sparring on your first day. You will not be asked to do anything your body is not ready for. The techniques are taught progressively — you start with the fundamentals and build up gradually, with plenty of repetition and individual attention.

We have students in their forties, fifties, and sixties who started as complete beginners. Some had never done any sport or martial arts before. They now train regularly, have learned real skills, and are enjoying the physical and mental benefits that come with consistent practice.

What to Expect in Your First Class

Knowing what to expect can make all the difference. Here is a step-by-step look at what your first class at Confluence Martial Arts will be like:

Arrival and Welcome

You will arrive at Cleveleys Citizens Hall, a welcoming and well-equipped venue. You will be greeted by Craig or one of the senior students, shown where to change, and introduced to the class. There is no formal ceremony to worry about — just a friendly welcome.

Warm-Up

Every class begins with a warm-up. This includes light stretching, mobility exercises, and some basic movements to prepare your body for training. Nothing extreme — just enough to get the blood flowing and loosen up the joints.

Basic Techniques

You will learn fundamental movements from the Daito-ryu jujutsu curriculum. These might include basic stances, footwork, and simple joint control techniques. Everything is explained step by step, and you will practice with a partner who is also learning. We pair beginners with more experienced students who can guide you through the movements safely.

Partner Drills

You will work through structured drills with a partner. These drills teach you how to apply techniques against a cooperative partner, building your understanding of distance, timing, and body mechanics. The emphasis is on learning, not on competition.

Cool-Down and Questions

Class ends with a cool-down and a chance to ask questions. You will leave having learned something new, having moved your body, and — we hope — with a smile on your face.

Two Arts, One Path

At Confluence Martial Arts, we teach two complementary traditional Japanese martial arts. As a beginner, you will be introduced to both, giving you a well-rounded foundation.

Daito-ryu Jujutsu

Daito-ryu is the art of joint locks, throws, and strikes. It is a sophisticated system that uses an opponent's energy and momentum against them. The techniques are elegant, effective, and deeply satisfying to learn. You will develop a keen sense of body mechanics and an ability to control situations without relying on strength.

Takeuchi Renshinkan

Takeuchi Renshinkan is one of the oldest Japanese martial systems, dating back to the 16th century. It encompasses jujutsu, weaponry (including sword and short staff), and self-defence techniques. Training in Takeuchi Renshinkan gives you a broader perspective on martial arts and connects you to a living tradition that spans centuries.

Learning both arts together is a unique opportunity. They complement each other perfectly — Daito-ryu develops your sensitivity and control, while Takeuchi Renshinkan builds your understanding of distance, timing, and weapon awareness.

Why Start Now?

The best time to start martial arts was ten years ago. The second best time is today. Every class you take builds on the last. Every technique you learn opens the door to the next. The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step — and that step is walking through the door for your first class.

Here is what you will gain:

  • Practical self-defence skills — techniques that work in real situations
  • Improved fitness — strength, flexibility, and cardiovascular health, built gradually and sustainably
  • Mental clarity — the focused, meditative quality of traditional training is a powerful antidote to a busy mind
  • Community — a supportive group of people who share your interest in learning and growing
  • Confidence — the quiet, genuine confidence that comes from knowing you can handle yourself

What About Your First Visit?

We welcome new students to get in touch to arrange a visit because we believe the only way to know if martial arts is right for you is to try it. There is no obligation, no membership fee to sign, no hidden costs. You simply come along, take part in a class, and see how it feels.

If you enjoy it, great — we can talk about how to continue. If it is not for you, that is fine too. At least you will have tried something new, and that is always a good thing.

"Every expert was once a beginner. The only difference between someone who trains and someone who wishes they trained is the decision to start. That decision is the hardest part — and once you make it, everything else follows."

— Craig Abernethy, Confluence Martial Arts

Location and Practical Details

Classes are held at Cleveleys Citizens Hall, FY5 3NG, in Thornton-Cleveleys. The venue is easily accessible from Blackpool, Fleetwood, Poulton-le-Fylde, and all parts of the Fylde Coast. There is free parking available, and the hall is fully accessible.

For your first class, wear comfortable clothing you can move in — tracksuit bottoms and a t-shirt are perfect. You do not need any special equipment. Bring a bottle of water and an open mind.
